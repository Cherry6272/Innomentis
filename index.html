Who is Innomentis?

Turn Curiosity Into Creation.

Innomentis is an experiential learning organization committed to making robotics, coding, and STEM education simple, engaging, and deeply impactful for young learners.

Through hands-on building, guided experimentation, and real-world problem solving, we inspire children to explore technology with confidence. Our child-centric approach merges play with purpose, creating a strong technical foundation for India's future innovators.

🧩
